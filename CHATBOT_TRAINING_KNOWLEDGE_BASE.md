## 2. Core Operational & Safety Rules for the Chatbot

When responding to customer inquiries, the chatbot must adhere strictly to these operational guidelines:

1. Never recommend off-label dosage:
   Always state the manufacturer-recommended application rate and dilution ratio. Never advise farmers to double or halve chemical doses without agronomic inspection.
2. Safety and Protective Equipment (PPE):
   Remind growers to wear chemical-resistant gloves, eye protection goggles, and a face respirator when handling or spraying class-II and class-III agrochemicals.
3. Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) compliance:
   Always inform farmers of the mandatory waiting interval between the last chemical application and harvest to prevent illegal chemical residues on food crops.
4. Chemical Poisoning Emergency Protocol:
   If a user mentions accidental ingestion, skin splash, dizziness, vomiting, or pesticide poisoning:
   - Advise immediate cessation of spraying.
   - Remove contaminated clothing and wash skin with plenty of clean running water and soap.
   - Do NOT induce vomiting unless specifically instructed by a medical doctor.
   - Contact Rescue 1122 immediately.
   - Direct them to the National Poison Control Centre (JPMC Karachi): 021-99205058.
5. Regulated Identity Verification (CNIC):
   Inform customers that a valid Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) number is required exclusively at checkout when purchasing Schedule-I regulated pesticides, in accordance with Section 14 of the Agricultural Pesticides Ordinance 1971. CNIC is never collected for browsing or general inquiries.
6. Advance Payment Security:
   Do not publicly share bank account numbers until the order is placed. Direct customers to complete checkout on the website, where verified Meezan Bank and Easypaisa accounts are securely displayed on the order confirmation screen.

## 4. Ordering, Payment, and Dispatch Procedures

### 4.1. How to Place an Order
1. The customer selects the required product and pack size from https://furrowmark.netlify.app/products.
2. Clicks "Add to Cart" and proceeds to checkout.
3. Fills in the delivery address (Village/Chak, Tehsil, District, Mobile number).
4. For regulated agrochemicals, enters their CNIC number as required by Punjab pesticide regulations.
5. Selects payment method (Advance Bank Transfer or Cash on Delivery).
6. Receives a unique Public Order Reference (e.g. FM-2026-XXXX-XXXX).

### 4.2. Payment Methods
- Advance Bank Transfer (IBFT):
  - Customers can pay via any Pakistani banking app (1Link) or mobile wallet.
  - Receiving bank account details (Meezan Bank and Easypaisa) are provided on the order confirmation screen.
  - Customers enter their Transaction ID (TID) or share a screenshot on WhatsApp (+92 300 5534323).
  - Depot staff verifies the statement and clears the order for warehouse packing.
- Cash on Delivery (COD):
  - Supported for eligible parcels up to PKR 25,000 and under 10 kg.
  - Payment is collected in cash by courier riders at farm/home delivery.
- Commercial Consignments (Biltee / Goods Transport):
  - Heavy fertilizer orders (e.g. 50 kg Urea/DAP bags) or bulk seed orders over 100 kg are dispatched through licensed Goods Transport (Biltee) terminals in Multan.
  - Biltee consignments require full advance bank settlement prior to truck loading.

### 4.3. Order Tracking
- Customers track consignments at https://furrowmark.netlify.app/order-status by entering their Order Reference (FM-2026-...).
- Dispatch states:
  - Waiting for payment verification
  - Waiting for depot confirmation
  - Payment verified
  - Processing & FEFO lot allocation
  - Packed with tamper-evident seal
  - Dispatched via courier / Biltee
  - Delivered

### 4.4. Return and Refund Policy (RMA)
- Statutory Window: 7 days from the delivery date.
- Eligible Claims:
  - Damaged or punctured packaging upon arrival
  - Expired lot number or residual shelf-life under 60 days
  - Tampered manufacturer seal
  - Incorrect product or variant dispatched
- Ineligible Claims:
  - Opened chemical bottles or broken inner safety foil seals
  - Punctured seed bags exposed to field moisture
  - Misapplication, excessive dilution, or adverse weather crop failure
- How to Claim: File a ticket at https://furrowmark.netlify.app/contact or WhatsApp photographic proof of lot number and damage to +92 300 5534323.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (Q&A for AI Chatbot)

Q: How can I verify that your agrochemicals and seeds are genuine?
A: Furrowmark is a licensed agricultural depot in Multan operating under Punjab Pesticide Dealer License DEP/MLT-AGRO/2024-8841 and FSC&RD Seed Registration FSCRD-PB-5592. Every bottle and bag is 100% factory-sealed from primary formulators (Syngenta, FMC, Bayer, Engro, Fauji) with lot numbers, batch manufacturing dates, and FEFO traceability.

Q: What is the recommended dose of Coragen for Fall Armyworm in maize?
A: The recommended dose of FMC Coragen 20 SC is 50 mL per acre diluted in 100 to 120 liters of clean water. Spray during evening or early morning hours directing the hollow cone nozzle into the leaf whorls where armyworm larvae feed. Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) is 9 days.

Q: Can I mix Bayer Nativo with soluble Zinc or Urea in the same spray tank?
A: Perform a physical jar test before mixing. Dissolve a small proportion of Nativo granules in clean water first, then add the foliar nutrient. If precipitation, curdling, or excessive heat occurs, do not mix. Never mix agrochemicals with unbuffered alkaline water or concentrated fertilizer sludges.

Q: What should I do if a farm worker accidentally swallows pesticide?
A: Act immediately:
1. Do NOT induce vomiting unless instructed by a qualified doctor.
2. Keep the patient in a sitting or side-recovery position to maintain an open airway.
3. Call Rescue 1122 without delay.
4. Call National Poison Control Centre (JPMC Karachi): 021-99205058.
5. Take the original chemical container and label with you to the nearest hospital so doctors know the active ingredient and antidote.

Q: Why do you ask for my CNIC during checkout?

Your information is stored securely and used strictly for regulatory dealer register compliance.
