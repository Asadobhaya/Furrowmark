# Name proposal

## Recommendation: Furrowmark

A furrow is the line a plough cuts for sowing. A mark is a boundary or a sign of quality in the trade sense of a hallmark. Together the word suggests a cut line in a field, which fits seeds and inputs without claiming anything about quality.

Why it fits the design rules:

- One word, easy to read and spell in Latin script, short enough for a wordmark in a plain typographic logo.
- Makes no claim ("best", "organic", "trusted"), so it does not conflict with the copy rules.
- Works as a plain wordmark for the favicon set (a lowercase "f" or "fm" in the primary green on a solid square), so no illustrated logo is needed.
