## Design tokens (proposal, owner can override with brand colours)

Defined in one file in Phase 1. Proposal: primary deep green, neutral grey scale, one restrained action accent, flat solid fills, 8 px grid, one corner radius. Exact hex values are chosen in Phase 1 and each text and background pair is checked for WCAG 2.2 AA contrast by a test. No gradient, no shadow heavier than a 1 px border.

## Banned content checks (automated from Phase 1)

A scan script fails CI on: gradient functions, emoji ranges, the em dash and en dash characters, "Made with" and builder or AI tool strings, banned copy words, `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` without sanitiser, `TODO`, lorem ipsum. Extended to rendered HTML in Phase 8.
